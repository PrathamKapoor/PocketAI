About PocketAI

A portable, offline AI assistant. The model, the search index, and every conversation stay on this machine.

Thinking styles

StyleWhat it does
Auto (default)Detects what you need and picks the right style automatically.
FastQuick answers for simple questions.
BalancedNormal tasks: everyday questions, explanations and advice.
Deep ThinkComplex reasoning: trade-offs, risks and second opinions.
ResearchAnalysis and comparison, ending in a clear recommendation.
BuildCoding and creation: working code, fixes and debugging help.

Developer mode

PocketAI works through internal stages and shows you only the final answer. Enable this to see which thinking style and internal stages ran behind each reply, plus model, hardware and memory status.

Known limitations

  • Single-slot inference: one answer at a time, queued.
  • CPU-only on typical hardware — expect roughly 8–10 tokens/second.
  • A 4B-parameter model: great for everyday tasks, not for frontier-level reasoning.
  • Targets machines with 8 GB RAM; low memory blocks inference by design.
  • No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry — and no web access from the model.

◆ Welcome to PocketAI

A portable AI assistant that runs entirely on this machine. No internet, no accounts, no data leaves the device.

  • Chat — type a question or paste a screenshot (Ctrl+V)
  • Images: paste from clipboard or click the Image button
  • Thinking styles: Fast, Balanced, Deep Think, Research, Build — or leave it on Auto
  • Documents: upload PDF/TXT/MD files and ask questions about them
  • Demo tab: sample documents, prompts, and a sample conversation
